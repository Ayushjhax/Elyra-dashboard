## 1. What Elyra is

Elyra is a tool for reading and understanding your own crypto and market
activity. It:

- shows your wallet, and the balances and positions on venues you connect;
- shows market data, research, signals and prediction-market analytics;
- answers questions with an AI assistant, Ask Elyra, using live tools;
- lets you set price alerts that notify you;
- prepares swaps and transfers for you to review, which you sign in your
  own wallet.

## 4. Your wallet and your keys

- Elyra never holds your private keys. An embedded wallet is created and

- Elyra builds a transaction, you review and sign it in your wallet, and
  Elyra submits it. Nothing is signed without you.
- **Transactions on a blockchain are final.** We can't reverse, cancel or
  refund one, including one sent to a wrong address.
- You're responsible for keeping your login, device and wallet secure, and
  for exporting an embedded wallet's key if you want to use it elsewhere.

## 6. Venue connections are read-only

Connecting Hyperliquid, Binance or Polymarket lets Elyra read balances and

An alert never trades. Don't rely on an alert alone to manage risk.
