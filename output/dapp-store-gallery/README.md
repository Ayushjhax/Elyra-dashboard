# Elyra — Solana dApp Store gallery

Three coordinated landscape PNGs, each exported and verified at exactly **1920 × 1080 pixels (16:9)**. The ZIP contains only the three finished images.

1. `01-markets-1920x1080.png` — Markets, in focus. Based on a fresh SOL asset/chart capture.
2. `02-ask-elyra-1920x1080.png` — Answers, with context. Based on a fresh Ask Elyra conversation with a sourced three-bullet SOL market brief.
3. `03-research-1920x1080.png` — Research, within reach. Based on a fresh Research / Market recap capture with Solana, Bitcoin and Ethereum prices and 24-hour moves.

Download bundle: `elyra-solana-store-3-images.zip`.

## Source captures

The connected Solana Seeker was opened through ADB. Installed package: `xyz.getelyra.app`, versionName `1.0.1`, versionCode `7`. Native screenshots are 1200 × 2670 pixels and are retained in `captures/`.

Selected originals:

- `captures/01-markets-sol.png`
- `captures/02-ask-sol-brief.png`
- `captures/03-research-recap.png`

Other captures document intermediate navigation states and are not final deliverables. The market-brief starter and a concise follow-up were submitted to Ask Elyra to capture a useful real reply. No financial transactions were made.

## Editing

The built-in imagegen tool created matching framed screenshot presentations using the actual captures, the existing forest/citron artwork and the official gold emblem. Editing added the landscape layout, device framing, restrained lighting and short feature captions. The phone screens are imagegen-rendered presentations of the captures; the raw device images are retained for comparison. The final PNGs were visually checked and exported at the exact dimensions with macOS sips.

Generation prompts: `prompt-01.txt`, `prompt-02.txt`, `prompt-03.txt`.

Captured market values and timestamps are historical screenshot content, not current quotes or performance claims. The set depicts existing Markets, Ask Elyra and Research surfaces; it does not add the earlier teaser's proposed future capabilities.
